Module

Website Radar

Scan candidate business websites and score how “upgradeable” they are for LegacyRefactor.ai services.

Run scanner
cd ~/opportunity-radar python3 website_radar/website_radar.py
Inputs
  • inputs/websites.csv – business name + URL list
Outputs
  • outputs/website_leads_YYYY-MM-DD.csv
  • outputs/website_leads_YYYY-MM-DD.json
Lead scoring Design age Mobile / SEO / structure
Module

Outreach Generator

Turn scored leads into ready-to-send outreach in three formats: cold email, short DM, and a quick audit blurb.

Generate outreach bundle
cd ~/opportunity-radar python3 website_radar/generate_website_pitch.py
Uses
  • Top rows from outputs/website_leads_*.csv
Creates
  • outputs/pitches/pitches_YYYY-MM-DD-HHMM.txt
Each lead includes
  • Cold Email – longer, more context
  • Short DM – quick, social-style pitch
  • Audit Bullets – copy-paste notes or tweet-length summary

Workflow: run radar → run outreach generator → copy the format you need into Gmail, DMs, or your outreach system.
